About Company

Intimate Techno Hub (ITH) began its journey in September 2021, headquartered in Dhaka, Bangladesh, with a vision to empower businesses through innovative IT and software solutions. Since its inception, ITH has been committed to helping organizations embrace technology, improve operational efficiency, and accelerate digital transformation.

Our initial focus was on delivering comprehensive information technology solutions tailored to the diverse needs of modern businesses. Over time, in response to evolving customer demands and emerging technological trends, we expanded our services to provide a broader range of IT solutions, including software development, system integration, IT consultancy, and digital transformation support.

At Intimate Techno Hub, we believe technology is not just a tool but a strategic driver of growth. By combining innovation, expertise, and client-centric approaches, we strive to deliver reliable, scalable, and future-ready solutions that help businesses achieve sustainable success.

Driven by a commitment to quality and continuous improvement, ITH aims to be a trusted technology partner for organizations seeking excellence in the digital era.
