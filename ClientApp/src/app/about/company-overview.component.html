About Us

Intimate Techno Hub (ITH) began its september in 2021 with a vision to deliver reliable, innovative, and comprehensive IT solutions to businesses and individuals in Bangladesh.

We specialize in providing a wide range of technology solutions, including Computer Hardware, Networking Solutions, CCTV Surveillance Systems, and IT Equipment Supply. Our goal is to help organizations build secure, efficient, and technology-driven workplaces through quality products, professional services, and dependable technical support.

With a strong commitment to customer satisfaction, we focus on understanding each client's unique requirements and delivering customized solutions that meet their needs. From supplying essential IT equipment to designing, implementing, and maintaining IT infrastructure, we strive to be a trusted technology partner at every step.

At Intimate Techno Hub, we believe that technology is more than just products and services—it is a powerful tool for growth, security, and progress. That is why we are committed to building long-term relationships through honesty, quality, innovation, and dedicated customer service.
