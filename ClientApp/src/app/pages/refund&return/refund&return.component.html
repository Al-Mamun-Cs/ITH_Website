REFUND & RETURN POLICY

    Refund
    ● To be eligible for a refund, the customer(s) must return the product(s) within 15 calendar days from the delivery date to the Many e-shop office. The product(s) must be in the same condition as they were received or delivered.
    ● Customer(s) can claim refunds within 30 calendar days from the date of delivery. After 30 days, you will not be eligible for a refund.
    ● The money will be refunded with the original payment method or Many e-shop’s preferable payment method on the invoice amount of the returned product.
    ● Refund request will be accepted if Many e-shop’s fails to deliver the product on time. (1-5 Days Inside Dhaka; 3-10 Days Outside Dhaka).

    Return
    Return will be accepted only-
    ● If the product is wrongfully delivered and different from what was offered, then returns will be accepted and will be exchanged with the correct/ordered product.
    ● If the product is dead on arrival, you must be informed within 1 day after receiving the product.
    ● If a valid warranty/guarantee card or sales invoice is available.
    ● If the product(s) is returned in the original product packaging.
    ● If returned items are not worn out.

    Return request will not be accepted if-
    ● The products are damaged due to misuse of the product.
    ● Product warranty period expired/warranty card code does not match the product code.
    ● Tempered product.
